Application of the 12 Principles of Animation

How Ollie Johnston and Frank Thomas's core animation principles are engineered into the film.

01 story.py

Squash and Stretch

Dynamic scale and vertical compression applied during impact landings, footsteps, and reactive character movements while preserving volume.

02 story.py

Anticipation

Preparatory crouch poses, subtle posture shifts, and pre-action wind-ups signaling major character turns and leaps to the viewer.

03 engine.py

Staging

Camera framing, headlight beams, atmospheric rain contrast, and focal lighting direct attention cleanly to the narrative subject.

04 story.py / engine.py

Straight Ahead & Pose to Pose

Key narrative poses are defined along timeline intervals while atmospheric rain, light flicker, and particle systems evolve straight-ahead per frame.

05 story.py

Follow Through & Overlapping Action

Secondary elements and limb motions settle at staggered intervals after primary body momentum comes to a halt.

06 engine.py

Slow In and Slow Out

Smoothstep and cubic easing interpolation curves govern camera pans, character acceleration, and scene fade transitions.

07 story.py

Arcs

Trigonometric sine/cosine trajectories guide natural limb swings, head tilts, and airborne motion paths rather than rigid linear shifts.

08 story.py

Secondary Action

Subtle blinking, breathing cycles, brow shifts, and environmental reflections reinforce emotional tone without distracting from main dialogue.

09 build.py / audio.py

Timing

Exact timestamp pacing controls dramatic pauses, dialogue delivery, and beat-matched sound cues across the 2–3 minute runtime.

10 story.py

Exaggeration

Amplified facial expressions, widened eyes, and heightened silhouette reactions clarify emotional beats at a glance.

11 engine.py

Solid Drawing

Consistent character proportions, layered depth parallax, perspective road lines, and grounded contact shadows maintain spatial believability.

12 story.py / engine.py

Appeal

Cohesive nocturnal color palette, expressive character design, and readable visual storytelling create an engaging cinematic atmosphere.
